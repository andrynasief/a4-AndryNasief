Orbit

Aim, drop, merge. Fill the glass without spilling over the rim.

  • Move the mouse (or drag on touch) to aim — a ring on the floor shows where the sphere will land.
  • Click or tap to drop the current sphere into the container.
  • Two spheres of the same size and color merge into the next size up when they touch.
  • Keep the stack below the rim line. If it stays over the line too long, the run ends.
  • Open Controls in the top right to tune gravity, bounce, and more while you play.
